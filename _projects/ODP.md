As a class, we were tasked to create solutions to help the grape industry be less harmed by the rise of spotted lantern flies (SLFs). Currently, SLFs are an issue, as they get caught in the grape harvest byproduct, causing batches of grapes to be thrown away, which significantly hurts profits and the economic viability of growing grapes. Below is my group's (the Grape Guardians) one-page product pitch that will be sent to members of the grape industry to hear their feedback.
